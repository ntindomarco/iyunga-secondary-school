ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Iyunga Technical Secondary School

Welcome to Iyunga

Iyunga Technical Secondary School is a government secondary school located in Mbeya City, Tanzania. The school provides secondary education together with technical and vocational skills.

Our goal is to provide quality education, develop practical skills and prepare students for further education, employment and responsible citizenship.
