About the School

Iyunga Secondary School

Iyunga Technical Secondary School is a government educational institution located in Mbeya City Council, Mbeya Region, Tanzania.

The school combines secondary education with technical and vocational skills development, preparing students with academic knowledge and practical competencies.

  • Registration: S.63/S0112
  • Region: Mbeya
  • District: Mbeya City Council
  • Ownership: Government
  • Type: Boarding School

Admissions & Joining Information

Information for students and parents regarding school selection, joining instructions and reporting.

Student Admission Process

  1. Check the official student selection information.
  2. Confirm Iyunga Technical Secondary School as the selected school.
  3. Obtain the appropriate joining instructions.
  4. Prepare the required documents, uniform and school supplies.
  5. Report to school on the required date.

Important Documents

  • Student selection letter
  • Joining instructions
  • Academic certificates/documents
  • Medical information where required
  • Passport-size photographs where required
  • Required school uniform
